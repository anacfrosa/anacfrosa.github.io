About Me

A bit about me

I graduated from the University of Aveiro with an Integrated Master’s in Computer and Telematics Engineering, where I built a solid foundation across software engineering, distributed systems, and network security. Along the way, I developed a particular interest in designing scalable systems and robust infrastructures.

During my internship at Apside (now CGI), I co-developed an IoT-based meeting room scheduling system with real-time e-paper displays, working within an agile team across the full development lifecycle.

For my master’s thesis, I designed a microservices-based intelligent kitchen assistant, integrating a PyTorch-powered prediction model to optimize inventory management and reduce waste in automated kitchens.

Currently, I work as a Full Stack Software Developer at Wish and Cook, where I maintain and evolve both the public platform and the maintenance software for the company’s autonomous kitchens, while also managing network infrastructure and integrating LLM-based tools to streamline backend development and data workflows, helping the team move faster and more efficiently.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me with a good coffee, spending time with my dog, or enjoying board games, running, and swimming.
